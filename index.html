FinTech · Full-Stack

Ethio Exchange

Ethiopian Bank Exchange Rates & Currency Converter

Problem
Buying and selling rates differ between Ethiopian banks and change frequently, so comparing them before converting money means visiting many bank pages by hand.
Solution
A platform that collects exchange-rate data from Ethiopian banks, stores it, and presents buying and selling rates side by side with conversion, rankings and per-bank pages.
Technology
React, TypeScript, Node.js, REST APIs, Supabase / PostgreSQL, Playwright, web scraping, Vercel, Render.
Result
A live, database-backed application for comparing bank rates, converting currency and browsing individual bank rate pages.

  • Supabase / PostgreSQL

Building an end-to-end machine-learning application where a trained image classifier is genuinely usable from a browser, rather than only in a notebook.
